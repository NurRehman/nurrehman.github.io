About

Fourteen years of production scars.

I've spent my career inside large-scale frontend systems — fraud and dispute platforms at Mastercard, banking systems at Citi, and now the digital storefront of a global airline at Qatar Airways. The recurring lesson: architecture is mostly about the decisions you avoid, and the best system is the one the next team can actually maintain.

I write and make videos to pass on what production taught me — bluntly, and without the enterprise jargon.
